## What to know about this form

- It is long. Choose it for a harder exercise.
- It has six sections. Section 5 holds most of the fields.

  | Section | Fields |
  | --- | --- |
  | 1. Business Name Information | 6 |
  | 2. Contact Information | 7 |
  | 3. Address (if applicable) | 48 |
  | 4. General Details (if applicable) | 1 |
  | 5. Authorization | 264 |
  | 6. Confirmation | 2 |

  The other four fields are hidden and are not questions.
- Section 5 does not fit in the day. In Lab 1 step 6, choose two or three of the other sections
  and ask Copilot to mark the rest `not today`.
- Mandatory questions have an asterisk in their label. The form definition does not flag them.
- Section 3 shows and hides address blocks from the answers: in Ontario or not, then a standard
  address or a lot and concession address, or Canada, U.S.A. or International.
- The form checks the email addresses, the telephone number and the company key.
- The outline lists the first 30 options of a dropdown list and ends a longer list with `...`.
  That cuts the 14 State and Country lists: take the full lists from the PDF.
