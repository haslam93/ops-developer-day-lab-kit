## What to know about this form

- It is the shortest sample form.
- It names two people, the applicant and the joint applicant. Each has the same address block.
- The oath is sworn in person. Its fields and the signatures are read-only in the PDF.
- The last section is completed by the marriage licence issuer, not by the applicant.
- The form checks the names, the postal codes and the date of issue.
- No field is flagged as mandatory in the form definition.
- The outline cuts long text at 300 characters. That cuts the notice of collection at the end of
  the form: take its wording from the PDF.
